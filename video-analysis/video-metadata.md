# Video metadata and analysis workflow

## What the reference video is

The attachment is a **phone screen recording of a native mobile banking app** (filmed with another phone, not a clean screen capture). It is not a website, so this brief is applied to a phone-width app: there is no sidebar or data table, and the equivalents are a bottom tab bar, bottom sheets and list rows.

Quality caveats: 480p, compression blur, uneven lighting and glare, a finger covers the tapped control in most interactions, no audio of interest, no tap indicators. Absolute colors depend on camera exposure (see `design-tokens.md`).

## Frame-analysis workflow (what was actually done)

1. **Metadata**: ffprobe (above).
2. **Systematic sampling**: 110 frames at 2 fps (every 0.5 s, t = 0.0–54.5 s). Each was rendered on a timestamp-labelled contact sheet (10 per sheet, 11 sheets) and **all 110 were reviewed**.
3. **Scene-change detection**: `select=gt(scene,0.045)` gave 37 timestamps (listed in `contact-sheets/scene-change-timestamps.txt`). Hand movement causes false positives, so the cuts that matter are 11.7–12.0 s (Home to Transactions), 15.88–15.98 s (sheet opens), 17.4–17.6 s (sheet closes), 26.6–26.8 s (back to Home), 34.9 s (Home to Deposits).
4. **Dense bursts at full 30 fps** around the four transitions that matter for motion: 11.20–12.23 s (screen change), 15.75–16.35 s (sheet open), 17.10–18.03 s (sheet close and list refresh), 35.90–36.60 s (spinner to content). 102 frames, reviewed on contact sheets (`dense_*.jpg`).
5. **Full-resolution inspection** of 12 individual frames (home, transactions, sheet, deposits, FD details at several scroll positions, nominee) for text, icon and layout detail.
6. **Color sampling**: median colors in hand-picked boxes of 6 frames, normalized against the in-frame white background (see `design-tokens.md`).

**Not claimed:** every one of the 1,651 frames was *not* inspected. About 212 frames (110 sampled + 102 dense) were reviewed, i.e. about 13% of the video, chosen to cover every state change. Transitions shorter than 0.5 s outside the four bursts (for example the reveal toggle and the carousel snap) were seen only at 2 fps.

## Output of the analysis

`frame-inventory.csv` has 138 rows: 110 sampled frames plus 28 key frames from the dense bursts. It is regenerated by `tools/make_frame_inventory.py`.
